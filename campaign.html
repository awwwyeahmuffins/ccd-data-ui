Tip: click a column to sort; Shift-click adds a secondary sort. Click a precinct on the map to find its row. Party counts are the modeled party universe, not actual ballots — unknown values stay blank.

How to read the numbers: the Win number is half the precinct's real past ballots plus one; the Vote gap subtracts your modeled supporters from it, so it compares a real ballot target against a modeled universe — use it to size the job, not as an exact headcount. Net vote opp. is your modeled supporters who sat home at the last big election (your get-out-the-vote gap) — sort by it to rank door-knocking, not by raw supporter count.
